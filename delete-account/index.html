Delete your Wazirly account

Wazirly, the Android app by Sharkstack Developments

You can delete your Wazirly account and the data linked to it at any time, with or without the app.

In the app

Open Wazirly, then Profile → Your data → Delete account. Profile is the round button at the top right of Wazirly's home screen.

It deletes everything listed below at once, on every phone.

If you never signed in, the same place shows Delete my data, which deletes the data linked to your phone instead (see the privacy policy).

Without the app

Email a deletion request to info@sharkstack.dev with the subject "Delete my Wazirly account". Send it from the email address you sign in to Wazirly with. If it comes from another address, we email the account's address to confirm before deleting anything.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • your email sign-in;
  • your reply settings;
  • your reply memory and saved replies;
  • your usage counts;
  • your Premium purchase record;
  • comments you sent with "Tell us more".

Usage statistics

If you agreed to usage statistics, they are linked only to a random identifier on your phone, never to your email, so an emailed request can't find them. To delete them, tap Delete account in the app, or sign out and tap Delete my data.

What is kept, and for how long

  • today's usage count for a Premium subscription, up to 2 days, so its daily limit still applies;
  • technical server logs, 30 days;
  • requests already sent to Anthropic, the AI provider, under its own retention (see Anthropic's data retention policy);
  • Crashlytics crash reports, which aren't linked to you, 90 days.

When

In the app, at once. For an emailed request, within 30 days, usually within 7. You'll get an email when it's done.

Premium

Deleting your account doesn't cancel Premium. To stop paying, cancel it in Google Play subscriptions.
