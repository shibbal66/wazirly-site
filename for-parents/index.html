Wazirly

A phone assistant for parents, grandparents, and anyone who calls you for help

Set it up once on their phone. From the makers of Wazirly, the AI phone assistant for Android.

The calls you get

"Is this text from the bank real?" "How do I send the photo?" "Where has the button gone?" If you're the family's phone support, you know them. The worry isn't the calls you get; it's the one you don't, the day a message looks real enough and you're not there.

What Wazirly does for them

  • A scam check before they tap. One button, on every screen, that reads a suspicious text or email and says in plain words what the warning signs are and what to do.
  • Replies written for them. Three ready answers for any chat, in their tone, in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.
  • Where do I tap. They say what they want to do and Wazirly rings the next button, screen by screen, without ever tapping for them.
  • Big, plain answers. Long emails and bills summarised, screens translated, questions about what's in front of them answered.

Setting it up once

  • Install Wazirly from Google Play on their Android phone and tap Start. Android asks to let Wazirly show its button over other apps and see the screen when tapped; say yes to both.
  • Choose their language: English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.
  • Put the button where they'll see it, and show them once: tap it, pick Scam check. That's the whole lesson.
  • It's free to start, with no card. While Wazirly is in early access, testers get Premium free.

What you get is fewer panicked calls, and the knowledge that the next convincing message meets a second opinion before it meets their bank card.

Questions

Does it work on an older phone?

Any Android phone with Android 8.0 or newer, which is nearly every Android phone in use today. It isn't on iPhone, because iPhone doesn't let apps show a button over other apps.

Will it read their messages all the time?

No. It reads the screen only when they tap its button, and only that screen. Pictures are sent only if they agree. Nothing is sold, used for ads or used to train AI, and everything can be deleted at any time.

Can I join the waitlist for them?

Yes. Join with your own email and choose Android; the invite comes to you, and you set it up on their phone. If they use iPhone, choose iPhone and you'll hear first when that changes.
