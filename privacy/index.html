Wazirly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Wazirly is an Android app that helps you with whatever is on your screen. You tap a floating button and Wazirly reads the screen to explain, translate or suggest what to do. This policy explains what Wazirly does with your information. In short: Wazirly only looks at your screen when you ask it to, and it does not sell your data.

What Wazirly reads, and when

  • Your screen, only when you ask. Wazirly captures the screen only when you tap its button, and only after you allow screen sharing through Android's own consent prompt. Android shows a notification while screen sharing is on, and you can stop it at any time.
  • Text on the screen. Wazirly reads text from the captured screen using Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs entirely on your phone.
  • Not the system bars. In versions after 0.3.0, Wazirly leaves out the status bar (time, battery, signal and notification icons) and the navigation bar when it reads or sends the screen.

What stays on your phone

Screen captures are kept in memory only while Wazirly's panel is open for that screen, and are then discarded. Wazirly does not save screenshots to your phone's storage or gallery. On-device text reading does not send anything off your phone.

Wazirly stores a few small things on your phone: whether you agreed to the cloud features, a copy of your Premium status, small app preferences (for example, how many times a tip has been shown), the random install identifier its server uses, and crash reports waiting to be sent (see Crash reports). Google Firebase, which Wazirly uses for its server and crash reports, also keeps its own app tokens on your phone.

Cloud features

Some features need more help than your phone can give, for example a detailed explanation or an image description. When you use one of these features, Wazirly sends only what is needed for that request (the text read from your screen and, if the feature needs it, a smaller copy of the screen image, plus your question or goal) over an encrypted connection to Wazirly's server, which runs on Google Cloud. Each request also includes your phone's language setting, used only to choose the language of the answer. The server passes the request to Anthropic, the AI provider that produces the answer on Wazirly's behalf, and sends the answer back to you. The features that look at the picture of your screen ("Describe image", "Ask a question" and "Where do I tap?") send it only after you agree to it in the app the first time.

  • Requests are not used by Wazirly to build advertising profiles and are not sold.
  • Wazirly's server does not store your screen text, screenshots, typed questions or answers.
  • To enforce daily limits and prevent abuse, the server keeps daily usage counts linked to a random install identifier (for Premium, to the subscription). They are deleted automatically about 31 days after the day they count. The install identifier itself is kept on the server until you tap Delete my data.
  • Server logs hold technical details only, such as the time, the result and the network address a request came from. They never include your screen content and are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Anthropic's commercial terms do not allow it to train its models on Wazirly's requests. Anthropic deletes requests and answers within 30 days, unless they are flagged as breaking its usage policy (then kept for up to 2 years) or the law requires keeping them longer. See Anthropic's data retention policy.

Wazirly tells you in the app when a feature uses the cloud. If you do not use cloud features, nothing from your screen leaves your phone.

Crash reports

If Wazirly crashes or runs into an error, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains technical details only: what went wrong in the app's code, the app version, your phone model and Android version, details such as screen size and memory, a random Crashlytics install identifier, and short notes about what Wazirly was doing (for example "read screen" or how long a step took). Wazirly never adds the text or images from your screen, AI answers or anything you type to a crash report. Crash reports are kept for 90 days and are not used for advertising.

Sensitive screens

Please do not use Wazirly on screens showing passwords, one-time codes, card numbers or other secrets. Recent Android versions hide some of this information from screen sharing automatically, but not every app marks its sensitive fields.

Premium and purchases

Wazirly Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; Wazirly never sees your card or payment details. To confirm a subscription, the app sends Wazirly's server the purchase token Google Play gives it, and the server checks it with Google Play. The server keeps that token, linked to your random install identifier, while the subscription lasts, so it can give you Premium's daily limits and see when it renews or ends. The server deletes the token about 3 to 4 days after the paid period ends, even if you don't use Wazirly again. To delete it sooner, tap Delete my data. You can cancel at any time in Google Play.

Ads

Wazirly may in the future show ads to free users (Premium has none). If that happens, ads may be served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID according to Google's Privacy Policy. Where required, Wazirly will ask for your consent first. This policy will be updated before ads are added.

Permissions Wazirly uses

  • Display over other apps: to show the floating help button and answers on top of the app you are using.
  • Screen sharing (media projection) and a foreground service: to capture the screen when you tap the button.
  • Notifications: to show that Wazirly is running and let you stop it.
  • Internet: for cloud features and app services.

Sharing

Wazirly does not sell or rent your information. It shares information only with the service providers described above (Google Cloud to run Wazirly's server, Anthropic to answer your requests, Google Play for purchases, and Google Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports), or when required by law.

Your choices and deleting your data

Wazirly does not require an account. You can stop screen sharing at any time from the notification and revoke permissions in Android settings.

To delete your Wazirly data:

  1. In the app: tap Delete my data. If you don't see it, update Wazirly from Google Play first (versions 0.3.0 and earlier don't have it). This deletes, on Wazirly's server, the usage counts and Premium purchase record linked to your install and the random install identifier itself. On your phone, it clears your cloud-feature consent, the copy of your Premium status and app preferences. If you have Premium, today's usage count for your subscription is kept so its daily limit still applies. It is deleted about 2 days later or, if you use Premium again that day, about 31 days later like other usage counts.
  2. Premium stays active. Deleting your data does not cancel your subscription. Wazirly checks your purchase with Google Play again and keeps your Premium under a new install identifier, with a new purchase record. To stop paying, cancel the subscription in Google Play.
  3. Data on your phone: open Android Settings → Apps → Wazirly → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall Wazirly. This removes only what is on your phone: server records stay, and the server can then no longer tell which were yours. Tap Delete my data first.
  4. Automatic deletion, even without a request: usage counts about 31 days after the day they count, Premium purchase tokens about 3 to 4 days after the paid period ends, and server logs after 30 days.
  5. Crash reports are not linked to you and are deleted automatically after 90 days.
