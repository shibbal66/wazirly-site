Wazirly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Wazirly is an Android app that helps you with whatever is on your screen. You tap a floating button and Wazirly reads the screen to explain, translate or suggest what to do. This policy explains what Wazirly does with your information. In short: Wazirly only looks at your screen when you ask it to, and it does not sell your data.

What Wazirly reads, and when

  • Your screen, only when you ask. Wazirly captures the screen only when you tap its button, and only after you allow screen sharing through Android's own consent prompt. Android shows a notification while screen sharing is on, and you can stop it at any time.
  • Text on the screen. Wazirly reads text from the captured screen using Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs entirely on your phone.
  • Settings you choose, such as your preferred language, are stored on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Screen captures are kept in memory only while Wazirly works on your request, and are then discarded. Wazirly does not save screenshots to your phone's storage or gallery. On-device text reading does not send anything off your phone.

Cloud features

Some features need more help than your phone can give, for example a detailed explanation or an image description. When you use one of these features, Wazirly sends only what is needed for that request (the text read from your screen, or the screen image if the feature needs it, plus your question) over an encrypted connection to Wazirly's server, which runs on Google Cloud. The server passes it to an AI model provider to produce the answer and sends the answer back to you.

  • Requests are not used by Wazirly to build advertising profiles and are not sold.
  • The server keeps a short record of requests (time, a random app install identifier and usage counts) to enforce free usage limits and prevent abuse. It does not keep the content of your screen after the answer is returned, except in short-lived error logs, which are deleted within 30 days.
  • AI providers process requests under terms that do not allow them to use the content to train their models.

Wazirly tells you in the app when a feature uses the cloud. If you do not use cloud features, nothing from your screen leaves your phone.

Sensitive screens

Please do not use Wazirly on screens showing passwords, one-time codes, card numbers or other secrets. Recent Android versions hide some of this information from screen sharing automatically, but not every app marks its sensitive fields.

Ads and purchases

Wazirly may in the future offer a paid Premium plan through Google Play and show ads to free users. If that happens, purchases are handled by Google Play, and ads may be served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID according to Google's Privacy Policy. Where required, Wazirly will ask for your consent first. This policy will be updated before either is added.

Permissions Wazirly uses

  • Display over other apps: to show the floating help button and answers on top of the app you are using.
  • Screen sharing (media projection) and a foreground service: to capture the screen when you tap the button.
  • Notifications: to show that Wazirly is running and let you stop it.
  • Internet: for cloud features and app services.

Sharing

Wazirly does not sell or rent your information. It shares information only with the service providers described above (Google Cloud and AI model providers) to answer your requests, or when required by law.

Your choices and deleting your data

Wazirly does not require an account. You can stop screen sharing at any time from the notification and revoke permissions in Android settings.

To delete your Wazirly data:

  1. Data on your phone (settings and preferences): open Android Settings → Apps → Wazirly → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall Wazirly.
  2. Server-side records (usage counts linked to a random install identifier, and short-lived error logs): email shibbalfarooq66@gmail.com with the subject "Wazirly data deletion". All records linked to your install are deleted within 30 days. Error logs are deleted automatically within 30 days even without a request.

Children

Wazirly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
