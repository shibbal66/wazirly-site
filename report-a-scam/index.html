Report a scam: who to call, where to report

For the US, the UK, Canada and Australia. Checked against each service's own pages on 28 November 2026. Part of the Wazirly scam library.

Never use a phone number or link from the scam message itself. Use the numbers below, or the number on the back of your card.

I already paid: do this in the next hour

  1. Call your bank or card company on the number on the back of your card (in the UK, 159 reaches most banks). Say it's a scam, and ask them to stop or recall the payment and block the card. Speed matters: in the US in 2025, the FBI's Recovery Asset Team froze $146.6 million, with a 56% success rate, when people reported quickly (FBI IC3 2025 report).
  2. If you paid another way, call that company now: the crypto exchange or crypto ATM operator; the gift card's company (tell them a scammer has the card number and PIN); or the payment app, asking it to reverse the payment (FTC).
  3. Cut the contact. Hang up, stop replying, and remove any app they asked you to install.
  4. Change any password you typed or shared, and turn on two-step verification. If you gave ID numbers: in the US, IdentityTheft.gov; in Australia, IDCARE on 1800 595 160.
  5. Keep the evidence: screenshots, the numbers and names they used, the amounts, dates and the account or wallet you paid.
  6. Report it, using the table below.
  7. Expect a second scam. Anyone who then offers to get your money back for a fee is running a recovery scam.

Country notes

United States

  • The FTC takes reports of any scam at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Forward scam texts to 7726 (SPAM) or use your phone's Report junk option (FTC).
  • The FBI's ic3.gov takes internet crime reports. Include the phone numbers, websites and full payment details.
  • Scams using the Social Security name: oig.ssa.gov/report. Fake USPS texts: spam@uspis.gov.

United Kingdom

  • Report Fraud replaced Action Fraud on 4 December 2025 for England, Wales and Northern Ireland, and kept the number 0300 123 2040 (GOV.UK). In Scotland, report to Police Scotland on 101.
  • Forward scam texts to 7726 for free, and send scam emails or screenshots to report@phishing.gov.uk (NCSC). Texts pretending to be HMRC can also go to 60599.
  • 159 connects you to most UK banks (Stop Scams UK). If you were tricked into sending a bank transfer, your bank must usually reimburse you up to £85,000 if you claim within 13 months of the last payment; a £100 excess may apply (Payment Systems Regulator).

Canada

  • Report to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre online at reportcyberandfraud.canada.ca, or on 1-888-495-8501, Monday to Friday, 10 am to 4:45 pm Eastern time, and to your local police (CAFC).
  • Forward spam texts to 7726 (SPAM) so your phone company can block the sender (Canada Post).

Australia

  • Report scams to Scamwatch. If you lost money or information online, report to ReportCyber too.
  • IDCARE is a free identity and scam recovery service: 1800 595 160, weekdays.
  • Scams using the ATO's name: check on 1800 008 540 or send screenshots to ReportScams@ato.gov.au (ATO). Fake myGov messages: reportascam@servicesaustralia.gov.au (Services Australia).

Questions

Is it worth reporting a scam if I didn't lose money?

Yes. Reports help phone companies block senders and help police see patterns. The FBI asks people to report internet crime at ic3.gov whatever the amount, and forwarding a scam text to 7726 takes seconds.

Who do I call first if I paid a scammer?

Your bank or card company, on the number on the back of your card. They can try to stop or recall the payment, and speed matters. Report to the police or fraud centre after that.

Is Action Fraud still running?

No. Report Fraud, run by the City of London Police, replaced Action Fraud on 4 December 2025 for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. The phone number stayed the same: 0300 123 2040. In Scotland, call Police Scotland on 101.

Will I get my money back?

It depends on how you paid and how fast you act. In the UK, bank transfers lost to scams are usually reimbursed up to £85,000 if you report within 13 months. Card payments have their own protections. Crypto and gift cards are the hardest to recover.
