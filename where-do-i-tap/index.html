Where do I tap? Step-by-step help in any Android app

How Wazirly's guide works, from the makers of the AI phone assistant for Android.

The problem

Apps move their buttons. The setting you found last year is in a different menu now, the "cancel" link is three screens deep, and the help page describes a version of the app you don't have. So you ask someone, or give up.

How Wazirly shows the way

  • Say what you want to do, by typing or speaking: "turn off notifications for this group", "cancel my subscription", "find my last order", "block this number".
  • Wazirly reads the screen and puts a ring around the next thing to tap, with one line saying why.
  • Tap it yourself. Wazirly never taps for you: you stay in control and learn the route.
  • It follows you to the next screen and shows the next step, until it's done. If you wander off, it notices and helps you back.

Where it works, and where it can't

It works in apps: chat apps, email, shopping, social media, streaming, and most others. For your safety, Android hides Wazirly inside the phone's own Settings app and on some banking and password screens, so it can't ring buttons there. Everywhere else, the button is one tap away.

Wazirly reads the screen only when you tap, and the step-by-step guide is just that, a guide: if a step doesn't match what you see, it says so and tries again from the screen you're on.

Questions

Can I speak instead of typing?

Yes. Hold the Wazirly button and say what you want to do, in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.

Does it work in my phone's Settings?

No. Android hides apps like Wazirly inside the system Settings app, and on some banking and password screens, to protect you. In every other app it works.

Will it tap things for me?

No, by design. It rings the next button and you tap it. That keeps you in control and means nothing happens on your phone that you didn't do yourself.
