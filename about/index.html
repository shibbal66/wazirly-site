Wazirly

Who makes Wazirly

Wazirly is built by Sharkstack Developments, a small independent studio.

The idea

Most of us know someone who gets stuck on their phone: a suspicious text they aren't sure about, a message they don't know how to answer, a setting they can't find. Usually they ask the one person in the family who "knows phones", and wait. Wazirly puts that help on the phone itself: a small button that sits over every app, reads the screen when it's tapped, and answers in plain words.

It started with scam texts, because that's where guessing wrong costs the most. Then came replies, where-do-I-tap guidance, summaries and translation, because people kept asking for them. The rule for every feature is the same: it has to work over the app you're already in, without copying, screenshots or switching apps.

The person behind it

Shibbal Farooq, founder of Sharkstack Developments, builds Wazirly and answers the support inbox personally. Every reply to info@sharkstack.dev is read by a person, and tester feedback decides what gets built next.

How it's run

  • Founded: 2026. Wazirly is in early access on Google Play, with real testers using it every day while it's finished.
  • How it makes money: a free plan, and a Premium subscription through Google Play. No ads, and nothing about you is sold.
  • Privacy: Wazirly reads your screen only when you tap its button, sends pictures only with your permission, and never uses what it reads to train AI. The privacy policy says exactly what is kept and for how long, and you can delete everything at any time.
  • Languages: English, Urdu and Roman Urdu today; it reads and translates many more.
