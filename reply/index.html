Wazirly

An AI reply writer that works inside any chat

How Wazirly's Suggest a reply works, from the makers of the AI phone assistant for Android.

What it does

You're in a chat and you don't know what to say: a relative asking you to dinner, a buyer haggling on Marketplace, a manager asking for an update, a message in a language you read better than you write. Tap the Wazirly button, choose Suggest a reply, and it reads the conversation on your screen and writes three replies that fit it: a yes, a no, and something in between, each in your own tone, not a robot's.

Tap the one you like to copy it, paste it in the chat, change a word if you want, send. Nothing leaves the app you're in.

Make it yours

  • Shorter, warmer, more formal: one tap each, and the three replies are rewritten.
  • My point: tell it in a few words what you want to say ("can't make Friday, offer Sunday") and it writes the message around that.
  • It follows the chat you're in: family, friends, work or a seller, in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, matching how the conversation is already going.

Where it works

WhatsApp, Messages, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram, Facebook Marketplace, Gmail, Outlook, and any other app where the conversation is on your screen. Wazirly reads the screen only when you tap its button; it never watches in the background, and what it reads isn't sold, used for ads or used to train AI.

Questions

Will the replies sound like me?

They're written to match the conversation you're in, so a chat with your brother gets a different reply from an email to a landlord. If one is too stiff or too casual, the Warmer and More formal buttons rewrite all three; My point lets you give it the gist in your own words.

Does it send the message for me?

No. You pick a reply, it's copied, and you paste and send it yourself. You always see the message before it goes.
